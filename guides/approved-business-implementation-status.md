## 3. Proposed schema changes

M-1 to M-4 were approved and **generated** on 2026-10-31. They are applied only to disposable PostgreSQL test databases and are
verified there. M-1's `ck_quotation_issued_fields` was corrected after the PostgreSQL run. **M-7 `ContractDraftDiscard`** is
proposed for B-02 (see the verification report §7). Their final design differs from the proposal below
in these points:
* survey requests gain a `Closed` status and five reason codes;
* substitution differences are per-product item rows;
* `chat_message.retrieval_audience` was added;
* the asset period columns are `warranty_starts_at/ends_at` with a `warranty_source`.

See the M-1..M-4 report. **M-5 and M-6 remain proposals, not generated.**
